Project Overview: Bridging Computer Science and Climate Change

Our project examines the effects of emerging technologies from both a positive and negative perspective. By explaining computer science topics in an easily digestible way, we aim to advance the general public's knowledge on environmental sustainability.

Project Objectives

  • Non-Biased Information: Present clear, factual, and balanced insights that outline both the advantages and challenges of emerging digital technologies in addressing climate change.
  • Accessible Education: Translate complex computer science concepts into simple, understandable language and visuals, ensuring that audiences with varying technical backgrounds can easily grasp the material.
  • Visually Engaging Layout: Design an interactive and appealing interface using infographics, charts, and visual storytelling to reinforce learning and motivate proactive steps toward sustainability.

How Computers Impact the Environment

Computer technology is a double-edged sword. On one hand, technologies like machine learning and high-performance computing enable us to model complex climate systems and optimize resource usage. On the other, the growing energy demands of data centers and the surge in electronic waste raise environmental concerns. Our work focuses on informing about these challenges and benefits by balancing digital innovation with sustainability.
