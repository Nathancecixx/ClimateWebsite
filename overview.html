Project Overview

This project explores the intersection of computer science and climate change. We examine how emerging technologies are transforming environmental monitoring, renewable energy, and sustainable urban planning.

How Computers Affect the Environment

Computer technology has a transformative impact on the environment. On one hand, advanced data analytics, smart sensors, and IoT solutions help optimize resource usage and monitor climate conditions. On the other, the growing energy demands of data centers and increased electronic waste present significant challenges. Balancing innovation with sustainability is key to leveraging digital progress for a greener future.

Our Approach

Using interactive visuals and in-depth analysis, our project breaks down complex topics into accessible insights. By educating and inspiring proactive solutions, we aim to empower communities in driving positive environmental change.
